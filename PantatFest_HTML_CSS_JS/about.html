About Pantat Festival

A celebration of Zarraga's identity, creativity, and community spirit.

Celebrating the spirit of Zarraga

PantatFest is a festival showcase dedicated to sharing the culture, local pride, and community life of the Municipality of Zarraga, Iloilo. Through performances, activities, and gatherings, the celebration brings people together and welcomes visitors to experience the town.

This website serves as an information hub where residents, participants, and visitors can explore festival activities, announcements, competitions, and memories.

Information notice: Festival dates, historical details, and official program information should be confirmed with the Municipality of Zarraga before publication.
